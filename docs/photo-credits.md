# Sample photos in templates

The sample photos in `public/templates/photos/` are used inside the
October 2026 trend templates (`public/templates/collection/trend-2026-10.json`)
and the graphic-design and birthday templates
(`public/templates/collection/designers-birthday-2026-10.json`,
`public/templates/collection/posters-birthday-2026-10b.json`,
`public/templates/collection/birthday-themes-2026-10c.json`,
`public/templates/collection/creative-sectors-2026-10d.json`) and the
"1000+" collection (`design-palettes-2026-10e.json`,
`sector-carousels-2026-10e.json`, `promo-ads-2026-10e.json`,
`community-sports-2026-10e.json`, `menus-brand-2026-10e.json`,
`invitations-decks-2026-10f.json`).
Customers replace them with their own pictures in the editor.

Source: the "Sample Images Collection" (github.com/yavuzceliker/sample-images),
which states that its images were originally sourced from Pixabay and modified
(colour, filters, effects), and may be freely shared and used under the
Pixabay Content License. They are resized here (max 1200 px) and used only as
part of designs, never sold or offered for download on their own.

If a rights holder asks for a photo to be removed, delete the file and swap
the frame in the affected template for another photo or an empty frame.

# Illustrations in Collection 500

The colour illustrations in `public/templates/collection/art/*.png` (cake,
balloons, flowers, animals, gifts and so on) are from **Microsoft Fluent
Emoji** (github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji), released under the **MIT
License**, which allows commercial use, modification and redistribution as
long as the licence notice is kept. The notice is in
`public/templates/collection/art/LICENSE-fluent-emoji.txt`. They were
rasterised to 640 px PNGs. Face emoji and people are not used.

`texture-paper.jpg` and `texture-grain.jpg` are generated noise (no
third-party source). All other shapes, confetti, frames and patterns are
drawn as editable vectors by `scripts/templates_kit/`.

Fonts used by the designs are Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License or Apache
2.0) and are already served by the editor.
